## 5. Meeting Minutes Requirements

Meeting Minutes must be kept for all formal and informal project meetings, including:

Tutor Meetings;

Client Meetings;

Internal Team Meetings;

Meetings with other teams or relevant personnel.

Meeting Minutes must be saved and organised in the team documentation.

The meeting minutes should focus on recording:

Meeting Date and Attendees;

Key Discussion Points;

Decision Points;

Action Items;

Assignees;

Deadlines;

The person who proposed a suggestion or solution;

The final decision reached by the team;

The reason for making the decision;

The follow-up work that needs to be completed.

The team may use recording devices or AI tools to assist in preparing the meeting minutes, but consent must be obtained from all attendees before recording or using the relevant tools.
