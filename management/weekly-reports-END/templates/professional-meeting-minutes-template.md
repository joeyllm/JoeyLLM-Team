6. Agree on preparation and evidence for the next meeting.

## Publication quality gate

Before publishing, confirm all boxes:

- [ ] Metadata, attendees, objective, Sprint Goal and location are complete.
- [ ] Every previous open action has a current status and evidence or a documented reason.
- [ ] Every work claim is labelled accurately as planned, researched, implemented, tested, deployed or confirmed.
- [ ] Every material stakeholder feedback item has a recorded team response.
- [ ] Every final decision includes rationale and affected evidence/PBI.
- [ ] Every new action has one accountable owner, an exact date and objective acceptance evidence.
- [ ] Every blocker has an impact, mitigation, owner and escalation path.
- [ ] Every process improvement has a success measure, review date and linked PBI where required.
- [ ] Next-meeting preparation identifies required actions, evidence, decisions and presenters.
- [ ] Ticket/PBI titles are unique, specific and linked to the relevant feature or outcome.
- [ ] Names, dates, links and statuses have been checked; no placeholder remains.
- [ ] The minutes are a concise decision-and-action record, not a transcript.
